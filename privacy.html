Privacy Policy

Official transparency disclosures and data handling policies for False Arcade on Android and iOS devices.

App: False Arcade ID: com.bertoxic.fluga Developer: Oraezu Effective: Sept 29, 2026
Quick Summary (TL;DR)
✓ No account required: You never have to register, enter an email, or sign in to play.
✓ Game saves stay on your device: High scores, initials, and unlocked levels are stored purely in local storage.
✓ Google AdMob integration: The app displays in-game ads served by Google to support development, collecting pseudonymous device identifiers and coarse location.
✓ User opt-out control: You can reset or delete your advertising identifier on Android and opt out of tracking on iOS via Apple's tracking prompt.

1. Overview #

False Arcade is an offline-focused, minimalist skill game designed and published by independent developer Oraezu ("we", "us", or "our").

We respect player privacy. We do not operate custom user databases, login portals, or user telemetry endpoints. Your scores, arcade records, and settings remain isolated in local device storage. To finance ongoing game development and updates, False Arcade incorporates Google AdMob to display third-party advertisements.

2. Information Handled by False Arcade #

A. Information We Do NOT Collect Directly

False Arcade does not directly request, access, or store personal user information, including:

  • Personal Identifiers: Real names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or national ID numbers.
  • Authentication Credentials: Passwords, account credentials, or social logins (Apple, Google, Meta).
  • Precise GPS Location: Real-time sensor-derived coordinates or physical location points.
  • Payment & Financial Information: Credit cards, bank accounts, or billing details (the app has no in-app purchases or subscriptions).
  • Biometric & Sensory Hardware: Camera feeds, microphone recordings, Face ID, or fingerprint sensors.
  • Device Files: Photo libraries, documents, or address book contacts.

B. Information Collected Automatically by Google AdMob

To display banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads, False Arcade integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob). Google AdMob automatically collects and processes:

  • Device & Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID on Android), Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA on iOS, subject to user tracking permission), or vendor identifiers (IDFV).
  • Approximate / Coarse Geolocation: General region (country, state, or city level) derived from IP address to deliver geographically relevant ads.
  • Ad Interaction & Telemetry: Ad impressions, view duration, click-through events, and video completion status.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Device manufacturer, OS version, carrier, network connection type (Wi-Fi or cellular), and crash logs related to ad rendering.

3. Data Processed Locally on Your Device #

To preserve game state across app restarts without requiring cloud accounts, False Arcade stores non-personal game data locally using standard system APIs (SharedPreferences on Android, NSUserDefaults on iOS):

  • Arcade Hall of Fame: Up to 3 retro arcade initials of your choice (e.g. AAA, VEX), numeric scores, and local timestamps.
  • Campaign Progression: Unlocked level tiers, high score benchmarks, and achievement medals.
  • Player Preferences: Audio toggles (sound effect volume, chiptune soundtrack) and haptic vibration preferences.

Device Isolation Guarantee: Your gameplay scores, initials, and preferences stay on your physical device. We never transmit this game data to our own remote servers.

4. Third-Party Advertising: Google AdMob #

We partner with Google LLC ("Google AdMob") to serve advertisements in False Arcade. Depending on your geographic region, device settings, and privacy choices, AdMob may serve:

  • Personalized Advertising: Ads targeted based on your previous browsing or app activity inferred from your advertising identifier across third-party apps and websites (subject to consent in applicable jurisdictions).
  • Non-Personalized (Contextual) Advertising: Ads based solely on the current context of the app (e.g., arcade gaming) and general coarse location (derived from IP address), without using cross-app behavioral profiles.

For more details on Google’s advertising privacy standards, refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google Uses Information From Sites or Apps That Use Their Services
  • Google AdMob Privacy and Security Documentation

5. Device Permissions #

False Arcade requests only platform permissions required for tactile gameplay and ad delivery:

Android

  • android.permission.INTERNET — Required to load and render ads from Google AdMob servers.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Enables the Google Mobile Ads SDK to verify network availability before making ad requests.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — Standard permission granting access to the Google Advertising ID for ad frequency capping, fraud prevention, and personalized ads (where permitted).
  • android.permission.VIBRATE — Triggers tactile vibration during gameplay collisions, scoring events, and fever modes.

iOS / iPadOS

  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): When requested on iOS 14.5 or later, Apple’s standard tracking authorization prompt (NSUserTrackingUsageDescription) is presented. If you select "Ask App not to Track", Google AdMob serves contextual ads and will not track your IDFA.
  • Core Haptics / Taptic Engine: Provides tactile feedback during arcade action.
  • No Sensitive Entitlements: The app does not request Camera, Microphone, Photo Library, Contacts, or GPS Location.

7. Privacy Controls & Ad Opt-Out #

You have full control over advertising identifiers and personalized ads on your device:

Android Devices

  • Open Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Tap Delete advertising ID to permanently remove your persistent ad identifier, or tap Reset advertising ID to wipe past advertising history.

iOS Devices (iPhone & iPad)

  • When prompted with Apple’s App Tracking Transparency dialog upon launching False Arcade, tap "Ask App not to Track".
  • You can modify this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking by toggling False Arcade off.

European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom

For users located in the EEA or UK, False Arcade displays an in-app consent form (Google User Messaging Platform / UMP) allowing you to consent to, customize, or decline personalized advertising and device identifier use in accordance with GDPR and the ePrivacy Directive.

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

Under California law, users have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of their personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can exercise this right through the device settings described above or through in-app consent preferences.

8. Data Retention & Deletion #

Data is retained according to its storage location:

  • Local Game Progress: Stored on your device until you clear app storage via OS settings (Settings → Apps → False Arcade → Clear Storage) or uninstall the application. Uninstalling removes 100% of locally saved scores, initials, and preferences.
  • Advertising Data: Managed by Google according to standard retention schedules (typically aggregated and anonymized after specified periods). Review Google's Data Retention Policy for details.

9. Children's Privacy #

False Arcade is designed for general audiences and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and global child protection standards.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). When False Arcade is configured for children or when a user is identified as being under the applicable age, Google Mobile Ads SDK is instructed to treat ad requests as child-directed (using Google's tagForChildDirectedTreatment flag), which disables personalized behavioral advertising and interest-based profiling.

10. User Rights & Regulatory Compliance #

  • GDPR / UK GDPR (Europe): The legal basis for processing local game state is contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The legal basis for personalized advertising is user consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); for non-personalized advertising and fraud prevention, it is legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You may withdraw ad consent at any time.
  • CCPA / CPRA (California): We do not sell personal information for money. Transfers of pseudonymous identifiers to ad networks may constitute "sharing" under CCPA; users may opt out via device settings or privacy prompts.
  • Non-Discrimination: We do not discriminate against any player for exercising their privacy choices.
