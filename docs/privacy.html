01 Introduction & Architectural Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the False Arcade mobile application (Package Identifier: com.bertoxic.fluga), created, developed, and published by independent developer Oraezu ("we", "us", or "our").

False Arcade is an offline, minimalist skill-game suite designed for Android and iOS/iPadOS devices. The application is built upon a strict Privacy-by-Design principle: we believe that a game does not need to harvest your personal information, follow you across the internet, or run background analytics in order to deliver an engaging arcade experience.

02 Information We Do NOT Collect

False Arcade does not collect, record, harvest, transmit, or share any of the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): Your name, email address, home address, phone number, government ID, or age.
  • Account & Social Credentials: Passwords, login tokens, or social logins (e.g. Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple, Facebook).
  • Geolocation Data: Precise GPS coordinates, cell tower data, or IP-derived geographical points.
  • Financial & In-App Purchase Data: Credit cards, bank accounts, or billing information. The app contains no in-app purchases, microtransactions, or paid subscriptions.
  • Biometric & Sensory Hardware: Camera streams, microphone audio, Face ID, or fingerprint sensors.
  • Persistent Advertising Identifiers: Apple IDFA (Identifier for Advertisers), Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID), or persistent device fingerprints.
  • Device Content: Your contacts, photo albums, files, or documents stored outside the app's sandboxed container.

03 Information Processed Locally on Your Device

To maintain your gaming state between sessions, False Arcade utilizes platform-standard local key-value storage (SharedPreferences on Android, NSUserDefaults / local container on iOS).

The following non-identifiable gameplay data is stored strictly on your local device:

  • Arcade Hall of Fame: Up to 3 retro arcade initials of your choosing (e.g., AAA, SAM, VEX) and corresponding high scores, levels reached, and local timestamps.
  • Campaign Progression: Unlocked difficulty levels, star thresholds, and achievement badges.
  • Audio & Tactile Preferences: Sound effect volume, chiptune music toggle state, and haptic feedback settings.
Strict Offline Guarantee: None of this information is ever uploaded to a remote server, shared with Oraezu, or made accessible to third parties. It remains safely sandboxed within your device hardware.

04 Device Permissions

False Arcade requests only the absolute bare minimum platform permissions necessary to deliver responsive tactile gameplay:

Android:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE Used exclusively to trigger the device haptic motor during edge collisions, game over states, and arcade fever modes.
  • Internet Permission: False Arcade deliberately does not declare android.permission.INTERNET in its manifest. The Android operating system enforces that the application has zero technical capability to initiate or accept network connections.

iOS / iPadOS:

  • Taptic Engine Utilized for tactile haptic feedback during gameplay.
  • Zero Sensitive Entitlements: Does not request permissions for Camera, Microphone, Photo Library, Contacts, Location, or App Tracking Transparency (ATT).

05 Third-Party Services and Analytics

False Arcade does not bundle, load, or initialize any external telemetry or monetization SDKs:

  • Zero Advertising SDKs: No Google AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin, ironSource, or similar ad networks.
  • Zero Analytics Platforms: No Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Mixpanel, Adjust, or AppsFlyer.
  • Zero Social Media Frameworks: No Meta/Facebook SDKs, TikTok SDKs, or social trackers.

07 Data Retention, Deletion & User Rights

Because all data is stored exclusively on your physical device:

  • Instant Erasure on Android: Open Settings → Apps → False Arcade → Storage & Cache and tap Clear Storage. This permanently purges all saved scores and settings.
  • Instant Erasure on iOS: Touch and hold the False Arcade icon on your home screen and select Delete App. iOS completely destroys the app's local sandbox storage.
  • Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v) Compliance: Because False Arcade requires no user accounts and maintains no remote database, an in-app "Delete Account" button is not applicable. Deleting the app or clearing device storage immediately destroys all associated user data.

08 Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)

False Arcade is family-safe and suitable for audiences of all ages. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in Europe.

We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or process personal data from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). In fact, because the app collects zero personal information from *any* player, children can enjoy the game in total privacy without risk of data harvesting or targeted tracking.

09 Global Privacy Rights

  • European Union / United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR): Local state processing is carried out solely for the execution of the requested game functionality (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Because Oraezu holds no personal data on servers, we cannot identify individual users.
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA / CPRA): We do not sell, rent, or share consumer personal information for monetary or valuable consideration ("Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information").
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against any user for exercising any of their privacy rights.
