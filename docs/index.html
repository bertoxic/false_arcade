Privacy Policy for False Arcade

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

Welcome to False Arcade ("the Application", "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), created and published by Bertoxic. We respect your privacy and are committed to ensuring complete transparency and security for all players.

This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you download, install, and enjoy False Arcade via the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store.

1. Summary of Key Principles

  • No Account Required: No registration, phone number, email address, or third-party sign-in is required to play.
  • Zero Remote Data Collection: We do not operate remote user tracking servers, databases, or analytics engines.
  • No Third-Party Tracking or Ads: The app contains no ad networks, no data brokers, and no tracking libraries.
  • 100% Local Device Storage: All scores, arcade initials, achievements, and settings remain strictly within your device's isolated storage sandbox.
  • Store Compliant: Fully aligned with Google Play Data Safety policies, Apple App Store Review Guidelines (App Privacy Nutrition Labels), GDPR, and COPPA.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

False Arcade does not collect, transmit, or share:

  • Personal Identifiable Information: Name, physical address, email, telephone number, or government identifiers.
  • Account Credentials: Passwords, login tokens, or social profiles.
  • Location Data: Neither precise nor approximate GPS/cellular/Wi-Fi location.
  • Financial Data: Bank accounts, credit cards, or billing info (False Arcade does not charge subscriptions or in-app purchases).
  • Sensory / Media: Photos, camera, microphone, or biometric identification (Face ID/Touch ID).
  • Advertising Identifiers: Apple IDFA, Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID), or persistent device fingerprints.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

To support arcade gameplay, False Arcade saves non-personal game states locally using platform-native secure key-value stores (SharedPreferences on Android, NSUserDefaults / local sandbox on iOS):

  • Leaderboard & High Scores: 3-character retro arcade initials (e.g., AAA, VEX), numeric scores, levels achieved, and local timestamps.
  • Game Progression & Unlocks: Campaign levels cleared, high score records, and challenge medals.
  • Game Settings: Audio preferences (sound effects volume, chiptune music toggle) and haptic feedback toggles.
Guarantee: This data remains strictly offline on your physical hardware. It is never transmitted across the network or shared with Bertoxic or any third party.

4. Device Permissions

Android

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides tactile feedback during gameplay collisions, scoring, and fever modes.
  • Internet Access: False Arcade does not declare the android.permission.INTERNET permission. The app has no technical capability to connect to the internet.

iOS / iPadOS

  • Taptic Engine / Haptics: Provides subtle haptic sensations.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: No permissions for camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, tracking (ATT), or background processing are requested.

5. Third-Party Services and SDKs

False Arcade does not bundle any third-party monetization or telemetry SDKs:

  • No advertising networks (e.g., AdMob, Unity Ads, AppLovin).
  • No analytics suites (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Adjust, AppsFlyer).
  • No external authentication services.

6. Data Retention and Deletion (Your Rights)

Because all data is stored strictly on your local device:

  • Clearing Storage: You can wipe all high scores and settings anytime via your device's application manager (Settings → Apps → False Arcade → Clear Storage on Android).
  • Uninstalling: Deleting the app from your device instantaneously and permanently deletes 100% of saved data.
  • Account Deletion Compliance: In accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v) and Google Play policy, since no user accounts exist and no cloud storage is utilized, no server account deletion request is necessary.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

False Arcade is family-friendly and suitable for players of all ages:

  • We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
  • We do not knowingly collect or request personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU).
  • Because zero personal data is collected from any user, no child data is ever captured or processed.
